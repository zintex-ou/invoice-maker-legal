Frequently asked

Where are my invoices stored?

On your device only. There is no account and no cloud copy on our side. If you switch phones, move your data with an iCloud backup or a direct device-to-device transfer before deleting the app from the old one.

How do I add my logo or bank details?

Open Settings → Business profile. Your name, logo, contact details, address and bank details appear automatically on new invoices and estimates.

How do I turn an estimate into an invoice?

Open the estimate and tap Convert. It becomes an invoice with the same client and items and gets the next invoice number.

How do I mark an invoice as paid?

Tap the status label (Unpaid) on the invoice and choose Paid. Paid and unpaid invoices are filtered separately and summed up in Reports.

I subscribed and features are still locked

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with, then tap Settings → Restore purchase. If that does not help, email us with the order ID from your Apple receipt.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On your iPhone open Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, or long-press the app icon and choose Manage Subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Because nothing is stored on our servers, that removes everything. There is no account to close.
